\chapter*{Declaration of the Use of AI}
\markboth{}{}

This declaration must include a description of all AI technologies used,
detailing which results they played a role in, and which parts of the dissertation
they were used for.

It is strongly recommended to refer to the current principles of the authoritative 
forums in the field regarding the use of AI in the declaration, 
to help readers and reviewers judge whether the thesis complies with these principles.

Spell checking does not need to be declared.
If no AI was used, you still need to include this section, but just declare
``No AI tools were used for the preparation of the dissertation.''
